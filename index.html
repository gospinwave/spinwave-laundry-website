Frequently Asked

Questions, answered.

Everything you might want to know before opening day.

When is Spinwave Laundry opening?

Spinwave Laundry is coming soon to San Pedro at 112 S. Pacific Ave. We don't have a public opening date yet — sign up using the Notify form below and we'll email you the moment we open.

Where is Spinwave Laundry located?

Spinwave Laundry is at 112 S. Pacific Ave., San Pedro, CA 90731, in the heart of downtown San Pedro.

What are Spinwave Laundry's hours?

We'll be open Monday through Sunday, 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM — seven days a week, 17 hours a day.

What sizes of washers and dryers will be available?

We'll have washers in 20 lb, 30 lb, 40 lb, 60 lb, and 80 lb capacities, plus high-speed dryers in 30 lb and 55 lb capacities. From a solo load to oversized comforters, every load has a machine sized for it.

Is there free parking at Spinwave Laundry?

Yes — there's ample free parking right outside the laundromat, well-lit and convenient day or night.

How do I pay at Spinwave Laundry?

We accept cash, credit cards, debit cards, and Apple Pay. Pay however is most convenient.

Is Spinwave Laundry attended or self-service?

Spinwave is a self-service laundromat that's also fully attended. Friendly staff are on site whenever we're open, so help is always nearby if you need it.

Does Spinwave Laundry have free WiFi?

Yes — free fast WiFi for all guests on every visit. Stream, scroll, or work while you wash.
