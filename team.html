02 — Maintenance routine

Tap a section to see the checklist.

Every evening Daily closing checklist
  • Remove lint from all dryer lint screens
  • Rinse out washer soap trays (scrub as needed)
  • Clean tops and fronts of all washers and dryers (including door glass)
  • Clean folding tables including bottom shelves and stools
  • Dust off bulkhead tops
  • Fill vending machine
  • Clean glass of vending machine
  • Clean bathroom — toilet, sink, floors
  • Check supplies in bathroom and refill as needed
  • Check supplies in customer hand sink and refill as needed
  • Take out all trash including bathroom and office (when needed)
  • Clean floors
  • Clean glass on both entry doors, spot clean interior windows
  • Dust off all black moldings / trim around the store (above and below metal walls)
  • Check that all machines are in working order — complete log and text Ricardo if anything is not working
  • Check if any carts, stools, etc. have gone missing — text Ricardo if so
  • Check parking lot for trash and sweep as needed
  • Check cleaning and vending supplies — text Ricardo / complete sheet as needed
  • Use stainless steel cleaner on all machines
Once a week Weekly deep checks
  • Vacuum front of vending machine
  • Vacuum backs of dryer lint trays
  • Dust and wipe metal around front windows
  • Check air freshener in bathroom
  • Open each bulkhead — check for trash and/or standing water (notify Ricardo immediately if water)
  • Check vacuum bag and change if needed
  • Dust tops of dryers
  • Clean out dumpster area
  • Move folding tables and clean / mop floor under
  • Water outdoor plants (more often during hot months)
  • Wash down front sidewalk and area near electrical panel outside
  • Clean floor drain in back near water tank
  • Clean utility sink in back
  • Vacuum behind dryers (every other week as needed)
  • Dust all signage and TV
  • Clean office floor
  • Clean off vending products so they don't have dust on them (cans, bottles, etc.)
Once a month Monthly deep clean
  • Remove hair and debris from wheels of carts using scissors or a razor blade
  • Deep clean dryer lint trays with vacuum
  • Deep clean washer soap trays
  • Dust ceiling and hard to reach areas
  • During summer: clean ceiling registers for coolers and surrounding ceiling tiles
  • Wipe out washer and dryer door seals
  • Clean off bathroom exhaust fan
Twice a year Biannual deep service
  • Remove lower front covers of washers and clean plastic trim — Ricardo to train staff
  • Remove tops of washers and clean around soap trays — Ricardo to oversee staff
03 — Customer help

When customers need help.

Let customers know — they're never alone.

Even if we're not on site, customers can call 1-888-XXX-XXX anytime. Our operators send a message straight to our cell phones and we text the customer back promptly. Most issues are resolved remotely within minutes.

Note: replace 1-888-XXX-XXX with the real answering-service number before opening day.
01Water leaking from a machine

Often caused by something stuck in the door seal. If the leak is minor, let the cycle finish. If needed, turn off the machine completely.

02Bathroom access

Scan the QR code with your phone and obtain the code.

03Card swiped or tapped, but the machine didn't start

Uncommon, but it can happen. Make sure the card is working and has credit or balance, and that the right machine has been selected.

04The vending machine didn't give the product

Sometimes these machines are possessed — no worries. Make sure the transaction happened, then open the vending machine and give the customer the product.

05"Slow Fill Error" message

Usually caused by too much detergent. Use fabric softener — pour a small amount directly into the detergent dispenser to quickly reduce excess foam.

06Too much soap

Overusing detergent is very common and prevents clothes from washing and drying properly. Tell customers to follow the posted soap guidelines.

07Clothes look dry inside the washer

If you don't see water, call us. The washer may be overloaded, with clothes absorbing all the water. Turn off and remove some items so the machine can wash, rinse, and spin correctly.

08The dryer didn't start after swiping

Call us. Also, confirm the customer pressed the start button for the correct dryer (upper or lower).

09Dryer shows 60 or 90 minutes

This means the card was swiped multiple times and time was added. To add more time, tap the card reader and press "Add Time."

10Lost card with remaining balance

No problem. Call us with the customer's name and phone number, and we'll issue a replacement card.

11Kiosk not working or cash not credited

Call us and we'll resolve it promptly.

12"I got a $10–$15–$20 charge on my card"

This is a temporary hold by the card company. It will drop off after the transaction settles.
