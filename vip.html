A note from Ricardo & Mariana

You may know this corner of San Pedro as Lucy's Laundromat. We're honored to take over a building with more than 104 years of community history and bring it back to life — this time with bright lighting, brand-new machines, and people on site who actually want to help.

The renovation is in full swing. We need a few more months to make it everything our San Pedro neighbors deserve, and we'll keep you posted every step of the way.

Follow @spinwavelaundry on Instagram and Facebook for live behind-the-scenes updates. Got a question, suggestion, or just want to say hi? Email us at info@gospinwave.com or stop by the storefront — we'd love to hear from you.
