Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Spinwave Laundry ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our website, sign up for our Text Club, or otherwise interact with us.

1. Information we collect

When you fill out a form on our website, we collect the information you provide directly, including:

  • Your name (first name)
  • Your mobile phone number (for our Text Club)
  • Your email address (optional)
  • Any message or notes you choose to send us

We also collect basic technical information when you visit our website (browser type, device type, approximate location based on IP) through standard analytics.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you the text messages, emails, and updates you signed up to receive
  • Tell you when we open, share grand-opening offers, and notify you of member-only promotions
  • Respond to your questions or comments
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. We don't sell or share your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not share your phone number with advertisers, data brokers, or any other business.

The only third parties who may process your data on our behalf are service providers strictly necessary to operate our website and messaging (for example, our hosting provider Netlify, our SMS sending provider if applicable, and basic analytics). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.

4. SMS / Text Club specifics

If you join our Text Club, you'll receive recurring promotional text messages from Spinwave Laundry at the phone number you provide. You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to any message we send. For help, reply HELP.

Message frequency varies but typically does not exceed 2–4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply depending on your wireless carrier and plan.

See our SMS Terms for the full terms of our text messaging program.

5. Your choices

  • Opt out of texts: reply STOP to any of our messages.
  • Opt out of emails: reply to any email asking to be removed, or email info@gospinwave.com.
  • Delete your data: email info@gospinwave.com from the email address associated with your account. We'll delete your information within 30 days.

6. Children's privacy

Our website and Text Club are intended for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we've collected information from a minor, we'll delete it.

7. California residents

If you live in California, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we have about you, request its deletion, and not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. To make a CCPA request, email info@gospinwave.com.

We do not sell personal information as defined under the CCPA.

8. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to function and may use analytics cookies (such as those provided by Google Analytics, if installed) to understand site usage in aggregate. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including transmitting form submissions over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated via text or email to subscribers.
